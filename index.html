About

Who is Callum?

I built The Kaizen from a free newsletter when I was 17. Got my first $1,000 commission sitting in a sixth-form photography class, then got shut down by the SEC for being under 18. Tried everything that didn't work — affiliate marketing, a failed agency, brand sponsorships — until a mentor pointed me back to the skill I already had.

Six years in, The Kaizen has helped 500+ professionals — from ex-corporate consultants to AI agency operators — package what they already know into real businesses. $2.7M+ in student revenue. 60,000+ newsletter subscribers. Both my parents retired (Dad runs ops with me now).

The methodology is simple: continuous improvement (kaizen) applied to monetisation. Small consistent steps, real skills, premium positioning. No copied business models, no hacks.

Philosophy

How I think about business

Sell what you already know

Most people already have a valuable skill. They just don't know how to package and price it. We had an intern go from £1,200 to £3,500/month selling the exact same skill to the exact same employer.

No copied business models

I wasted months trying to copy someone else's agency model. It doesn't work. Your business should be built around your skills, not someone else's template.

Speed over perfection

I've done speed runs going from zero to £10K in 37 days using the framework. Most people spend months planning and never launch.

Continuous improvement

Kaizen means change for the better. Not overnight success. Not hacks. Small, consistent steps compounding into real results.
